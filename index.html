Cloud Browser v2 — full control

Disposable Playwright Chromium in the cloud (this machine). Unlimited time — no limit at all, session ends only when you click Stop. Profiles keep separate logins (cookies + localStorage) — switch profiles to change accounts. No sign-up. Click the live view to focus it, then type directly into it, right-click, double-click, hover — everything goes to the remote browser.
